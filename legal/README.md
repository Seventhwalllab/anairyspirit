# Licence texts

The An Airy Spirit Licence Agreement, **Edition 1.0 (20 August 2026)**, in seven languages.

Every artifact carries, beside these files, [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE) — the short statement —
and [`EULA.txt`](../EULA.txt), the English edition in plain text. The files here are the
same agreement, clause for clause.

Each translation says in its own header that English prevails — **unless the law
applicable to the reader provides otherwise**.

## Editions

Section 13 makes the edition that came with your artifact the one that applies to you.
So an edition is never rewritten in place: a change means a new edition, and artifacts
already in the world keep the terms they were shipped with.
