# Licence texts

The Mehengram Licence Agreement, **Edition 1.0 (14 August 2026)**, in six languages.

The text that ships with an artifact is [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE) at the repository root —
the English edition in plain text, so it travels inside every package. The files here
are the same agreement, clause for clause.

## The texts

| file | language | role |
|---|---|---|
| [`EULA.en.md`](EULA.en.md) | English | **authoritative** — a dispute is read against this text |
| [`EULA.es.md`](EULA.es.md) | Español | Chile is the home market; consumer terms are read in Spanish there |
| [`EULA.fr.md`](EULA.fr.md) | Français | France (loi Toubon) and Québec require French in consumer contracts |
| [`EULA.pt-BR.md`](EULA.pt-BR.md) | Português (BR) | Brazil's consumer code, same reason |
| [`EULA.zh-Hant.md`](EULA.zh-Hant.md) | 繁體中文 | Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau |
| [`EULA.ru.md`](EULA.ru.md) | Русский | the language the wording was argued out in |

Each translation says in its own header that English prevails — **unless the law
applicable to the reader provides otherwise**. That second half is the one that matters:
several of the jurisdictions above require the local language for consumer terms, and
that requirement cannot be contracted away.

## Why these languages and not the largest ones

Not by number of speakers. Two different reasons run through the list:

* **Obligation** — Spanish, French, Portuguese. There the local language is a condition
  of the terms binding a consumer at all. This is also why Hindi and Arabic are absent
  despite the populations: no such requirement reaches us.
* **Reach** — traditional Chinese. Simplified was considered and left out: mainland
  enforcement of terms like ours is weak, and Singapore — the other simplified-reading
  market — runs its law and its business in English, which the authoritative text
  already covers.

## Editions

Section 13 makes the edition that came with your artifact the one that applies to you.
So an edition is never rewritten in place: a change means a new edition, and artifacts
already in the world keep the terms they were shipped with.
